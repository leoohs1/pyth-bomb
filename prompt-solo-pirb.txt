PIRB — PERSONAGEM DO MODO SOLO (Dungeon Run)
============================================
Regra da Pyth: a MÁSCARA do Pirb não pode ser alterada. Só pode mudar roupa, pose/expressão
do corpo e o ambiente. Sempre anexe a imagem de referência da máscara (as 5 vistas).


------------------------------------------------------------
P1) PIRB EM PÉ — anexe a referência da máscara
------------------------------------------------------------
Cute chibi game character sprite: a small person wearing EXACTLY the pigeon mask from the
reference image as their head. The mask must stay identical to the reference: same shape,
blue-gray textured skin, orange beak, orange eyes with white rims, white patch above the beak,
purple watermelon-striped helmet with the white Pyth "P" logo centered on the front,
the V-shaped helmet edge over the beak. Do not redesign, recolor or simplify the mask.
Chibi proportions: the mask-head is big (about 45% of the total height), small compact body.
Outfit (this part can be creative): ancient Greek hero style, a short white tunic with gold
trim and a purple sash, small purple cape, gold arm bands, brown leather Greek sandals.
Standing upright, full body, facing the viewer, confident friendly pose, fists on the hips.
Soft painted cartoon game-art style, clean outline, soft lighting from the top.
Centered, feet touching the bottom of the frame, nothing cut off, square 1024x1024.
Solid flat pure green background (#00FF00), no gradient, no shadow on the ground,
no green anywhere on the character, no text besides the "P" logo on the helmet.
